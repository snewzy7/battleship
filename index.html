          It's Battleship. Just snacks.

          1. Stock your pantry. Place five snacks on your grid (click a square, R to rotate, or "Stock randomly"). Your roommate hides theirs too.
          2. Take turns searching. Click a square on the roommate's pantry. A red X means you found part of a snack; a dot means an empty shelf. Then they search yours.
          3. Find every square of a snack to take it. Clear out all five of theirs before they eat all of yours.
